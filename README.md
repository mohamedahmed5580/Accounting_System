 🛒 Sales & Inventory Management System (POS)
A comprehensive desktop application designed to streamline retail operations, manage inventory, and handle financial transactions for small to medium-sized businesses. This system features a user-friendly Arabic interface with full support for barcode scanning, invoicing, and financial reporting.

Key Features
1. 📦 Inventory Management (المخازن والأصناف)
Product Master Data: Create and manage products with details like Code, Barcode, Name, Unit, and Expiry Dates.

Pricing Levels: Support for multiple pricing tiers (Purchase Price, Selling Price, Wholesale Price).

Stock Control: Real-time tracking of stock levels with "Reorder Point" alerts.

Inventory Adjustment: Tools for physical inventory counts and adjustments.

Barcode Integration: Generate and print custom barcodes for products.

2. 🧾 Sales & Point of Sale (المبيعات)
Sales Invoicing: Fast billing interface supporting barcode scanning and manual entry.

Sales Returns: Handle customer returns with automatic stock and cash adjustment.

Sales Representatives: Track sales rep performance and calculate commissions automatically.

Quotation: Create price quotes for customers.

3. 🚚 Purchasing & Suppliers (المشتريات والموردين)
Purchase Management: Record incoming stock from suppliers and calculate weighted average costs.

Purchase Returns: Manage goods returned to suppliers.

Supplier Database: Manage supplier contact details and bank account information.

4. 👥 CRM & Accounts (العملاء والحسابات)
Customer Profiles: Manage customer data, credit limits, and balance history.

Accounts Payable/Receivable: Track debts (Customers) and liabilities (Suppliers).

Transaction Recording: Record payments (Receipt Vouchers) and expenses (Payment Vouchers).

5. 💰 Financials & Reporting (المالية والتقارير)
Expenses Management: Track daily operational expenses (Rent, Electricity, Wages).

Profit & Loss: Analyze net profit based on sales and cost of goods sold (COGS).

Daily Journal: A complete log of all daily financial transactions.

VAT Support: Calculate Value Added Tax and discounts (Percentage or Fixed Amount).

🛠️ Tech Stack
Language: C#

Framework: .NET Framework (Windows Forms)

Database: SQL Server / LocalDB / Access [Choose yours]

Reporting: Crystal Reports / RDLC

UI Design: Custom UI with Arabic (RTL) support.
